# Field Signal — the Phytos design language

**The movement.** Field Signal is the aesthetic of a monitoring station after dark: the land is vast and unlit, and every point of light on the console is a fact someone verified. The page is not paper pretending to be a screen, nor a screen pretending to be paper; it is an instrument panel for agriculture, built by people who measure before they speak. Where the generic AI artifact is warm cream, soft copper and borrowed friendliness, Field Signal is dark ground, luminous signal, and earned trust.

**Space and form.** The canvas is deep moss-black, the colour of miombo woodland at night, and generosity of space does the work that decoration would otherwise fake. Elements sit on a strict grid with wide gutters; nothing touches, nothing crowds. Cards and panels are barely-lighter rectangles of the same darkness, their edges drawn with hairlines rather than shadows. Flow moves left to right and top to bottom like a survey transect: capture, assess, verify, act. Every alignment is the product of painstaking, master-level refinement; the composition should look as if someone moved each element a dozen times before letting it rest.

**Colour as signal, never as mood.** Three lights are permitted on the dark ground. Bone (#EDE9DD) is the voice: all reading happens in it. Leaf-signal chartreuse (#C9E265) is life and action: it marks what is growing, working, or must be done next, and it is spent sparingly, the way a ranger spends battery. Harvest gold (#D9A441) is attention: warnings, emphasis, the second line of a thought. Sage (#7D9184) murmurs the secondary labels. No other hue may enter; restraint is the craft. A single filled chartreuse block per page is the loudest permitted gesture.

**Type as instrumentation.** Three voices, each with a job. Bricolage Grotesque speaks the headlines: confident, slightly idiosyncratic, unmistakably human. IBM Plex Mono is the instrument readout: kickers, stats, labels, coordinates, legends — anything that states a measured fact wears the mono. Instrument Sans carries the body quietly at small sizes with open leading. Text is sparse and essential; the mono labels in particular are short, uppercase, letterspaced, like markings etched on equipment by a technician who expected the gear to outlive them.

**Craft.** The finished artifact must read as the work of countless hours: optically aligned, contrast-checked for sunlight and projector alike, every connector routed orthogonally, every label given breathing room. Nothing overlaps, nothing decorates, nothing is there because a template suggested it. The dark page among white pages is itself the signature — but the signature only holds if the execution is meticulous, deliberate, and obviously the product of deep expertise.

**Application.** Dark ground for documents and the intelligence dashboard (the night console). An inverted bone-light variant with the same three signals serves the farmer-facing app, which is used in direct sun. The system is one identity in two exposures: night for those who watch the whole country, day for those who stand in the field.
