# Dashboard patterns: what Phytos takes from six products

Research note, 29 Oct 2026, for the light-theme restyle of the Phytos Command Center (national map with "Districts to watch", alert list, outbreak detail, observation detail with evidence panel, officer verification queue, Outgrower View, knowledge sources, scenario page). Scope: OneSoil, John Deere Operations Center, Climate FieldView, xarvio FIELD MANAGER, Linear and Stripe Dashboard, studied only from public material (websites, help centres, docs, release notes, press and app-store screenshots) under a 60-minute cap. Where a detail could not be seen in public material the table says so rather than guessing. Some agri sources are dated (xarvio guides 2021, Deere release notes 2022, FieldView Cab screenshots 2018 to 2019); treat them as evidence of the pattern, not of the current release.

## Rules for Phytos

1. **Header identity.** The left of the header shows the organisation or workspace name with a chevron (for example "Phytos demo · Lusaka"); the right holds at most help, notifications and a round initials avatar that opens the account menu. No email address appears anywhere in the chrome, only inside the menu if at all. Test: render the header with a signed-in user and assert the email string is absent. (Stripe, Linear, John Deere, xarvio all name the org, never the email; the initials avatar is our choice, none of the six showed theirs publicly.)
2. **Sidebar nav.** Left sidebar, every item an icon plus a sentence-case text label ("Verification queue", not "VERIFICATION QUEUE" or an icon alone). Group headers small and sage. The sidebar sits on ground #F0EDE2 so the #FAF8F1 content panels lead. Active item: 3 px chartreuse bar on the left edge plus a light tint, label stays ink #1A2420. Test: no nav item without visible text; active item has both bar and tint. (Linear, Stripe for labels and case; Deere and OneSoil mobile for a solid active marker; OneSoil web as the counter-example.)
3. **Quiet counts.** Count badges only on queue-type items (Alerts, Verification queue), right-aligned, neutral fill with sage text, at most 3 digits ("99+"). Never chartreuse, gold or red. Test: badge background uses the neutral token. (Linear Inbox count, Deere "+1".)
4. **Map with a right-hand panel.** On the national map, outbreak detail and observation detail, the map fills the content area and the panel sits on the right at a fixed 360 px with a close control (and expand where the content is long). Detail opens in this panel, not in a modal; a blocking overlay is kept for the officer verify form only. Test: clicking a district opens the panel at 360 px with no backdrop. (John Deere right-hand panel, Stripe drawer and "default to ContextView", xarvio right detail panel, OneSoil 360 px panel.)
5. **Two-way selection.** Clicking a district on the map selects its row in "Districts to watch", and selecting a row outlines that district on the map; the selected row gets a deep-leaf tint, the map feature a deep-leaf outline. Test: select from either side and both highlight. (xarvio list tint plus map outline, OneSoil map click driving the panel, Deere row tint.)
6. **Legend and map controls on the map.** Layer and filter controls float at the top left of the map as label-over-value buttons; the risk legend sits inside the map at the bottom left and is always visible; zoom sits on the right edge. Test: legend element is a child of the map container. (OneSoil top-left controls and bottom-left legend, Deere colour options at the bottom of the map, xarvio lower-left legend; FieldView's legend-less yield map as the counter-example.)
7. **Status as dot plus word.** In lists and tables, status renders as a small coloured dot followed by the word (HIGH, ELEVATED, LOW; Verified, Pending review), never as a filled pill. Each status word has one fixed meaning across every view. Test: no filled status pills inside table rows. (FieldView dot plus word, Deere plain-text status, Stripe fixed badge meanings with text.)
8. **At most two chips per row.** A row in the alert list, verification queue or Outgrower View carries at most two chips (for example the alert class and the DemoBadge); anything more collapses to a muted "+N". Status filters with counts sit above the list ("All 12 · Pending 5 · Verified 7") rather than on every row. Test: count chips per rendered row. (xarvio one chip per row and count chips above the list, Deere "+1", OneSoil rows with no chips.)
9. **Two-line rows.** Every list row is at most two lines: the name in ink, then one sage secondary line joined with middle dots ("Chibombo · 4 reports · 2 verified"). Numbers right-aligned; tables of fields or farms end with a totals row. Test: no row renders a third text line at 1280 px width. (OneSoil name plus area, xarvio name plus one grey line, FieldView and Deere totals rows.)
10. **Wrap, do not truncate, in panels.** In the side panel, evidence panel and outbreak detail, text wraps; ellipsis is allowed only in dense full-width table cells, and then the full text is in a title attribute. Fields in panels use label above value, never label and value squeezed on one line. Test: no `truncate` class inside panel components. (Stripe list example wraps a long name and drawer lists stay "compact and focused"; Deere panel label-above-value.)
11. **8 px spacing scale.** Padding, margins and gaps use only 4, 8, 16, 24, 32 or 48 px (2 px for hairlines and focus rings). Test: no arbitrary Tailwind spacing values outside this set in `apps/web/src`. (Stripe spacing tokens.)
12. **One primary action per view.** Each view has at most one chartreuse filled button, placed at the top right of the view header; other actions are outlined or text buttons. Chartreuse is used only for that button and the active nav bar, never for selection, links or chips. Test: count chartreuse-filled elements per view. (John Deere single yellow "Add" with outlined siblings, Stripe "place action buttons in the header"; xarvio's all-blue chrome as the counter-example.)

Not adopted for lack of evidence: a specific max content width (none of the six states one publicly) and a fixed row height in pixels.
