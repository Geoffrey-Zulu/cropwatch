# UI review, 29 Oct 2026: Field Brief → Field Signal

The same 12 views before (this morning's deploy, navy and copper "Field Brief") and after (the "Field Signal" restyle and density pass). Same preview fixtures and the same viewports: the capture app at 390 px (phone), the Command Center at 1440 px. No view was re-flowed.

What changed:

- **Command Center: night console.** Dark ground, panels a step lighter, hairlines; Bricolage Grotesque headlines, IBM Plex Mono for every label and status, Instrument Sans body; one chartreuse figure per view.
- **Map.** Unlit land, Elevated and High districts glowing gold and red-gold, real reports as points of light, chartreuse cluster rings.
- **Density.**
  - Alerts are one-line signal rows: class, level, issue · district, reason, verification, age, detail.
  - Districts to watch are one line each.
  - Visit lists are one line per farm with reason chips (`CLUSTER`, `SEVERE`, `AWAITING REVIEW`, `HIGH DISTRICT`) in place of repeated sentences.
- **Capture app: day exposure.** Ground `#F0EDE2`, ink `#1A2420`; the wordmark on its dark chip; the headline one step smaller; the sticky primary button in chartreuse with dark text (quiet until it can be pressed).
- **Kept.** The advice ladder, the IPM order, the Evidence panel, confidence bands, the DEMO badge and every string.
