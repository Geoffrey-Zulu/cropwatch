Pitch-morning checklist

Start 2 h before the pitch
Laptop: repo at tag freeze-2026-10-29, pnpm 12.9.1
gcloud as zulugeoffrey034@gmail.com · run from the repo root

1. Warm the API 2 MIN

  • bash infra/scale.sh 1 → ok: min instances 1; {"status":"ok",…} (about US$2 a day until step 9)

2. Re-arm the live demo at the venue 5 MIN

  • Dry run: pnpm --filter @repo/seed seed --live-demo --demo-issue tuta_absoluta --demo-center=-15.388311,28.346069 --demo-district Lusaka
  • Good: 4 qualifying tuta_absoluta reports within 10 km, Armed until a time after the pitch, no Checks failed
  • Same command again with --commit

3. Rehearse the presenter's report, then undo it 5 MIN

  • pnpm --filter @repo/admin-tools outbreak-e2e --image leaf-mines.jpg --center -15.388311,28.346069 --district Lusaka
  • Good: All checks passed; report → dispatched under 30 s (8 s on 29 Oct). It cleans up after itself; the demo stays armed.
  • pnpm --filter @repo/admin-tools smoke-e2e --image leaf-mines.jpg --crop tomato → all checks passed, including the advice check

4. SMS (Africa's Talking) 5 MIN

  • Secrets in Secret Manager and deployed? If not, alerts go by push only and the SMS is logged: say so on stage
  • Account balance checked
  • Judges' phones subscribed with consent: sms-subscribe add --phone +260… --district Lusaka --consent "verbal, at the venue, 1 Nov"

5. Quota and spend 3 MIN

  • A 429 in step 3 means Gemini quota pressure: wait 10 min, re-run, keep the recordings ready
  • Billing → Budgets: cropwatch-hackathon well under its first 50% alert

6. Devices 10 MIN

  • Demo phone: Phytos installed from the capture URL, camera and location allowed, mobile data on (not only wifi), battery above 80%
  • The leaf: tomato with LEAF MINES. Photograph the mines close up in daylight. Not fruit (a fruit photo once came back as early blight)
  • Laptop: signed in to the Command Center as the agronomist, national map, focus Lusaka and Chongwe, projector tested (dark console reads?)
  • Kit: hotspot charged; backup recordings on the desktop and the USB stick; printed copy of this sheet

7. One full run at the venue, with a stopwatch 5 MIN

0:00P1: the problem1:15P2: officer queue, confirm the report, counter ticks, alert becomes a verified cluster alert
0:20P2: national map, DEMO badge visible1:35P2: Outgrower View, Prioritise field officers
0:30P1: camera, leaf mines, Tomato, Send; bridge line while it diagnoses1:55P2: Evidence panel (cut first if late)
0:55P2: report lands, cluster ring, model risk signal, phone buzzes2:10P1: close: who pays, what is next, the team
  • Practice reports near the venue complete the cluster: if this run used the real leaf near the venue, repeat step 2 (--commit) before the pitch

8. If it goes wrong on stage

  • "An officer will review": verify it in the queue; that completes the cluster
  • Wrong issue: "an honest miss: this is why officers verify"; correct it to Tuta absoluta
  • Wifi or API down: hotspot; still down, play that step's recording and keep talking
  • Slow diagnosis: keep talking over the map; it retries by itself. Over 10 s: recording

9. After the demo

  • bash infra/scale.sh 0 → min instances 0
  • Optional: pnpm --filter @repo/seed seed --commit (default demo data, not armed)

Never say

"predicts", "probability", a confidence percentage, "the model learns from corrections", "registered" for a PMDG product, "platform".
