### Postharvest Technology

The Unit focuses on generating, improving, and adapting a wide range of post-harvest technologies designed to minimize crop losses caused by storage pests and related factors. Its work includes developing improved storage structures, promoting safe and effective grain protection methods, and introducing technologies that enhance drying, handling, and preservation of agricultural produce. The unit also evaluates emerging post-harvest solutions to ensure they are suitable for local farming systems. Through these efforts, the unit helps farmers maintain the quality, safety, and market value of their crops long after harvest. Soil Microbiology unit also offers microbiological analytical services such as microbial count and soil respiration.
